Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions,
honest answers.

Can I lose fat and build a toned body at the same time?

It's possible to lose fat and build muscle at the same time, particularly if you're new to strength training or returning after a break. Your approach will depend on your starting point, experience, and goals.

Will lifting weights make me bulky?

Building substantial muscle takes sustained training and time. We'll structure your training around the shape, strength, and level of muscle development you want to work towards.

Can I target belly fat?

Exercises can strengthen your abdominal muscles, but they don't let you choose where you lose fat. We'll focus on overall fat loss and strength training to improve your body composition.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Your training plan will reflect your current ability and help you build confidence gradually.

Can I train at home?

Yes. We can discuss a home training approach based on your equipment and goals. Some equipment may be helpful as you progress.

How many days a week do I need to train?

We'll agree on a realistic schedule based on your goals, experience, and availability. Consistently following an achievable plan matters more than committing to a schedule you can't maintain.

Do I have to stop eating rice, carbs, or my favourite foods?

No. These foods can fit into your nutrition plan. We'll focus on your overall intake, protein, portions, and habits.

Will I need to count calories?

Not necessarily. We'll discuss whether tracking would help you or whether a different approach would be more suitable.

Do I need to do cardio every day?

No. Cardio and daily movement can support your fitness and fat-loss goals, but the amount should suit your overall training, recovery, and schedule.

How quickly will I see results?

That depends on your starting point, consistency, and goals. We'll set realistic expectations and review progress using more than your weight alone.

Is this online or in-person coaching?

This is online coaching. You complete your workouts independently, with a structured plan, guidance, and scheduled coaching check-ins.

What if I have an injury or health condition?

Include this in your application so we can establish whether my coaching is suitable. You may need clearance or guidance from a qualified healthcare professional before starting.

How much does coaching cost?

The available coaching options, duration, fees, and payment terms will be explained before you enrol. Complete the application to discuss your needs.
